At a Glance

Building Bridges Between Nepal & the World

Origin Nepal was founded in Kathmandu, Nepal, in 2012 and has grown to become one of the leading trading firms, committed to introducing innovative goods. We bring product markets together to support the needs of today by trading goods.

We are committed to make the transition to a more sustainable, affordable and reliable products for tomorrow. We do import and export. Our business spans industrial supplies and machinery, machinery and tools, apparels & garments, sports, toys, games & fitness, agriculture & farming, handicrafts & decorative and technologies.

This diversity provides us with the flexibility to adapt to changing market dynamics and the ability to provide a wide range of dynamic merchandises, wherever they need both nationally and globally.
