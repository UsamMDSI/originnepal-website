About Us

A Kathmandu Firm Built on Lasting Partnerships

Origin Nepal was founded in Kathmandu in 2012. We have grown to become a trusted trading firm committed to introducing innovative goods — from industrial supplies to handicrafts — both domestically and internationally.

Our business spans industrial supplies, machinery, apparels & garments, sports goods, agriculture & farming, handicrafts and technologies. This diversity gives us the flexibility to adapt to changing market dynamics and serve buyers nationally and globally.
